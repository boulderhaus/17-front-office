## Why it works
- **The voice is the product demo.** We hear the full live call, uncut. The agent sounds like a person: UK English, short turns, one question at a time, and it plays back what it heard ("new worktops and a new island about two by one metres").
- **It handles the price question the way an owner wants.** "Can I get a quick quote?" gets "I can't give an accurate price right now because Martin's going to need to look at the details. Once I pass this on he'll give you a call back and a free quote." Then it collects the name and confirms "is this the best number?"
- **A real tradesman in his real shop**, filmed POV. It's his reaction, not an actor.
- **Hook text plus captions.** "AI is getting out of hand 🙈" is curiosity and fear in five words, and the captions carry it on mute.
- **The flow overlay** shows how it works without anyone explaining it.
- **The payoff is the owner's text**, which is exactly Call Otto's core loop.
