# Product and distribution strategy

## Wedge

Sell the outcome against voicemail: “When you cannot answer, it captures the job, proves what happened, and gives you the callback.” Do not sell autonomy or staff replacement.

## Acquisition loops

1. Free missed-call text-back audit: connect/forward a number, measure unanswered calls, and show recovered requests. This creates proof before asking for full AI answering.
2. Evidence referral: every owner summary makes the value visible; a weekly “calls recovered, bookings proven, failures needing attention” report gives the owner a shareable artifact.
3. Jobber ecosystem: once the connector is proven, App Marketplace search and Jobber consultants are higher-intent distribution than generic AI ads.
4. Local trust: HVAC associations, distributors, bookkeepers, MSPs, and trade coaches already have the owner's trust. Offer flat referral economics, not affiliate hype.
5. Vertical templates: HVAC-first greeting, emergency rules, pronunciation, approved fee script, and service categories reduce setup and improve call quality.

## Retention

- Daily utility: calls and after-hours coverage recur without requiring a new habit.
- Switching cost earned ethically: owner-approved knowledge, rules, and evidence history—not number custody or inaccessible data.
- Trust compounding: confirm mode first; expose the proof; let the owner opt into direct booking only after observed success.
- Failure honesty: missed bookings, failed texts, ambiguous writes, and dropped calls become tasks instead of hidden errors.

## Pricing hypothesis

- Solo: $49/month, after-hours/overflow, message capture, text summary, Google bridge.
- Crew: $99/month, direct booking after verification, multiple owner destinations, expanded usage.
- Publish usage caps and overage before launch. Never silently disable coverage. No setup fee, annual lock-in, or percentage take.

These prices are a hypothesis to test against Jobber's $29 add-on and standalone products around $49–$199; they are not validated revenue claims.
